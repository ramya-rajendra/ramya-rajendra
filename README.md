## Executive summary

Most companies know their processes are slow. What they don't know is **where the time goes** and **which fix pays off first**. I find out by mapping the process, measuring it against the data, ranking the fixes, and putting a euro figure on each one.

### Zalando app reviews: what users want fixed first &nbsp;[→ repo](https://github.com/ramya-rajendra/zalando-app-review-analysis)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Question** | What frustrates Zalando app users most, and what should the product team fix first? |
| **Data** | 3,000 German Play Store reviews, GA4 funnel sample in BigQuery |
| **Answer** | About 55% of checkouts aren't completed. A more reliable checkout came out on top after RICE, and became a PRD and a [clickable prototype](https://ramya-rajendra.github.io/zalando-app-review-analysis/docs/checkout-prototype/) |

### Purchase-to-pay on real company data &nbsp;[→ repo](https://github.com/ramya-rajendra/p2p-real-data-analysis)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Question** | What delays supplier payments in a large company? |
| **Data** | BPI Challenge 2019 event log, 1.6 million process steps |
| **Answer** | Median 64 days from invoice to payment. Payment blocks hit 27.6% of cases and add about 10 days, and 73% are released by hand |

<img src="https://github.com/ramya-rajendra/p2p-real-data-analysis/raw/main/images/issue_impact.png" width="100%" alt="Extra days caused by payment blocks, changes and cancellations" />
